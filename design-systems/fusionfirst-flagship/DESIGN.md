# FusionFirst Studios — Flagship Design System

> Category: Enterprise SaaS
> Layered workshop dark with amber, green, red, and blue signals. Combined reference: wavespace.agency, digitalists SOTD, Redondo, Studio Simms, Linear.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

The flagship FusionFirst design system combines warm dark surfaces with amber as the earned accent, plus a full signal palette (green, red, blue) for data visualization. Editorial pull quotes and Linear-style data rows.

- **Visual style:** warm dark, editorial, data-dense
- **Color stance:** primary (amber), signals (green/red/blue), success/warning/danger
- **Design intent:** Keep outputs recognizable to this layered dark style while preserving readability.

## 2. Color

- **Canvas (BG):** #0A0807 — deep warm dark
- **Surface:** #16120E — raised container
- **Paper surface:** #F5F0E6 — editorial insert / light mode card
- **Text primary:** #F2EDE3 — warm off-white
- **Text secondary:** #B5AB99 — muted warm
- **Text muted:** #7A6F5E — captions
- **Accent (Amber):** #E8A04A — earned accent, primary CTAs
- **Signal green:** #5EBA82 — positive states, growth
- **Signal red:** #EB5757 — negative states, errors
- **Signal blue:** #6F8FE8 — informational
- **Border:** rgba(232, 160, 74, 0.08) — amber-tinted hairline

## 3. Typography

- **Scale:** desktop-first with clamp() for display (56px–144px)
- **Families:** display=Space Grotesk, body=Inter, mono=JetBrains Mono, serif=Newsreader
- **Leading:** body 1.6, tight 1.06

## 4. Spacing & Grid

- 4px base grid (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 192px)
- Section rhythm: 128px desktop / 80px tablet / 48px phone
- 1200px container with 36px/24px/16px gutters

## 5. Layout & Composition

- Editorial pull quotes with paper surface treatment
- Linear-style data rows with signal colors
- Clear content blocks with consistent padding

## 6. Components

- Buttons: amber for primary, signal colors for state-specific actions
- Inputs: amber focus ring, warm dark backgrounds
- Cards: raised elevation with amber ring border
- Data rows: signal-colored status indicators

## 7. Motion & Interaction

- Medium transitions (200–400ms) with natural easing
- Hover states darken accent by 8%

## 8. Voice & Brand

- Tone is confident, data-informed, editorial
- Keep microcopy action-oriented

## 9. Anti-patterns

- Do not introduce off-palette colors
- Do not flatten hierarchy
- Do not add decorative effects that reduce readability
- Do not use signal colors for non-data purposes
