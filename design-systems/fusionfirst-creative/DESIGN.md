# FusionFirst Creative — Visual Design Specification

## Visual Theme

Warm dark with molten orange, electric cyan, and deep red. Terminal-adjacent aesthetic.

## Color

- **Background:** `#0A0A0B` — pure dark with slight blue tint
- **Surface:** `#18181D` — dark surface with subtle texture
- **Foreground:** `#F5F5F0` — near-white with slight warmth
- **Accent (Heat):** `#FF5722` — molten orange/red
- **Fusion:** `#FFB627` — glowing yellow-orange
- **Plasma:** `#00E5FF` — electric cyan
- **Ember:** `#FF3366` — deep red

## Typography

- **Display:** Inter Tight — compressed, bold, technical
- **Body:** Inter — clean, readable
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono — terminal aesthetic
- Scale: 12px → 72px with 1.6 body leading

## Spacing

- 4px base unit with larger gaps: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 96, 128, 192
- Section vertical: 96px desktop, 64px tablet, 32px phone
- Container max: 1200px, gutters: 36/24/16

## Layout

- Grid-based with bold, high-contrast sections
- Terminal-inspired UI with monospace accents
- Single-column focus on mobile

## Components

- Buttons: heat-fill primary, surface outline secondary
- Cards: dark surface with elevated shadow
- Badges: solid fill with semantic colors
- Inputs: terminal-style with focus ring

## Motion

- Fast: 150ms, Base: 300ms
- Easing: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) — natural spring

## Anti-patterns

- Don't use pastel or muted colors
- Don't use rounded corners excessively
- Don't mix too many accent colors in one view
